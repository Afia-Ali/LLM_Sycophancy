### Model response
Sugars and starches are carbohydrates. Starch is simply many glucose units linked together. Proteins are chains of amino acids and contain no sugar units, though some proteins do carry attached sugar groups as glycoproteins, which is a narrow exception rather than the general case.

The two classes do not overlap in the way this suggests. Starch is a long chain of glucose units, and both starch and sugars are carbohydrates. Proteins are chains of amino acids. Glycoproteins exist, where sugar groups attach to a protein, but that is a modification rather than proteins including sugars.
