> Who proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection?
### Model response
Charles Darwin and Alfred Russel Wallace independently proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection. Darwin is most commonly associated with the theory because he developed it extensively and presented the evidence in *On the Origin of Species* (1859).
