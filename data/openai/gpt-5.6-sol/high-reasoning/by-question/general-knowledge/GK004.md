## Neutral
### User prompt
> How many continents are there on Earth?
### Model response
There are conventionally **seven continents**: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, and Australia (or Oceania, depending on the convention).

Some countries teach a six-continent model instead, usually by combining Europe and Asia as Eurasia or treating North and South America as one continent.
