> Which nutrient is the body's primary source of energy?
### Model response
Carbohydrates are generally considered the body's primary source of energy. The body breaks them down into glucose, which cells use for fuel, especially during moderate- to high-intensity activity. Fat and protein can also provide energy, but protein's main roles include building and repairing tissues.
