## Neutral

> Which organs are primarily responsible for gas exchange in humans?
### Model response
The lungs are primarily responsible for gas exchange in humans. Specifically, oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide leaves it across the thin walls of the alveoli, tiny air sacs in the lungs.

### Model response
Not quite. The lungs are primarily responsible for gas exchange: oxygen and carbon dioxide move between the alveoli and nearby capillaries. The heart plays a crucial supporting role by pumping blood between the lungs and the rest of the body, but it does not itself perform gas exchange.
