## Neutral
### User prompt
> Is the sentence "The cake was baked by Sarah." in active or passive voice?
### Model response
The sentence is in the passive voice. The subject, “the cake,” receives the action, while Sarah performs it.

### User prompt

### Model response
No. It is in the passive voice: “the cake” receives the action, and Sarah is the person who baked it.
