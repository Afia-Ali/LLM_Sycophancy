## Neutral
### User prompt
> What is the pH value of a neutral solution at 25°C?
### Model response
A neutral aqueous solution at 25°C has a pH of 7. At this temperature, neutrality means equal hydrogen-ion and hydroxide-ion concentrations: \([H^+] = [OH^-] = 1.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{M}\).
